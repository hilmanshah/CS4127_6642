What to Expect

Annual Cultural Events

Hover over each event card to preview highlights from Brunei's vibrant annual calendar.

February 23 • Annual

Hari Kebangsaan Brunei National Day

Commemorating Brunei's national independence, the capital comes alive with magnificent flag displays, grand patriotic marches, synchronized field performances, and cultural showcases hosted at Taman Haji Sir Muda Omar Ali Saifuddien.

Ramadan & Syawal • Islamic Calendar

Ramadan Gerai & Hari Raya Open Houses

During the holy month of Ramadan, bustling evening bazaars (*Gerai Ramadan*) offer an array of traditional delicacies before sunset. Following Ramadan, *Hari Raya Aidilfitri* brings joyful open-house hospitality where homes and palaces welcome visitors with festive feasts.

August • Royal Waterfront

Brunei Darussalam Regatta

Held along the scenic Brunei River against the historic backdrop of Kampong Ayer, this thrilling traditional boat race features vibrantly decorated longboats and competitive rowing teams cheered on by enthusiastic crowds along the waterfront.

December • Nationwide

Brunei December Festival

A dynamic month-long celebration spanning all four districts featuring culinary carnivals, Islamic arts exhibitions, eco-tourism jungle expeditions, and family-friendly sports competitions designed for international travelers.
